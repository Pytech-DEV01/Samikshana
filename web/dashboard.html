How to read these figures

Completed and recommended counts come from individual work exports. MP allocation and expenditure come from the MP summary. Transaction amount is aggregated from the expenditure export; these datasets may differ in reporting scope and should not be treated as a reconciliation.

RISK METHODOLOGY

Review indicators, not allegations

Delivery watch: completion rate below 25% where at least 10 works are recommended.

Payment watch: more than 10% of reported transactions are pending/in progress, with at least 5 transactions.

Utilization watch: expenditure is below 40% of recommended funds, with at least 10 recommended works.

Two or more indicators are labelled “Review”; one is “Watch”. The labels identify records for further review and do not imply fraud or misconduct.
